Genre: Physics Arcade / Procedural Action

The Idea: You never control the unicorn directly.

Instead, draw the physics around it using seven different rainbow powers. Redirect its momentum, launch it through obstacles, sling around anchors, grind across rails, flip gravity, warp through space or combine colours to create new interactions.

Your Goal: 

Keep the unicorn moving and descend as deep as possible through a constantly changing procedural world.

Build speed, smash through structures, collect coins and pigment, chain together colour abilities, and survive the increasingly chaotic descent.

Whatever happens, don’t let the unicorn stop.

The Seven Colours

🔴 Red — Push
🟠 Orange — Redirect
🟡 Yellow — Spring
🟢 Green — Tether
🔵 Blue — Rail
🟣 Indigo — Gravity
💜 Violet — Warp

Cross two coloured strokes to mix their powers.

Mouse Drag — Draw a physics stroke
1–7 / Mouse Wheel — Select colour
X — Release an active rail or tether
R — Restart
P / Esc — Pause
